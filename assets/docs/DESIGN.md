## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the sensory, bespoke world of ultra-luxury boutique tobacco craftsmanship. Rooted in Dominican heritage, vintage humidors, and atmospheric private member lounges, the design language marries tactile luxury with minimalist editorial precision.

### Personality & Demographics
- **Heritage & Ritual:** Rooted in patience, curation, fermentation, and small-batch rolling traditions.
- **Atmospheric & Intimate:** Evoking low amber lighting, Spanish cedar vitrines, leather armchairs, and aged spirits.
- **Audience:** High-end cigar aficionados, collectors, luxury lifestyle purveyors, and private club members who value subtlety, pedigree, and meticulous packaging.

### Aesthetic Movement
A hybrid of **Minimalist Editorial Luxury** and **Subtle Skeuomorphic Depth**. Clean structured grids, airy typographic hierarchy, and hairline framing are counterbalanced by deep tobacco-leaf values, warm cedar undertones, matte obsidian surfaces, and foil gold accents.

## Colors

The color palette reflects natural aging, tobacco leaves, warm humidors, and premium cigar bands:

- **Primary (`#D4AF37` - Vintage Gold Leaf):** Used for focal brand moments, crest monograms, active navigation highlights, and bespoke border accents.
- **Secondary (`#8A3C1B` - Maduro Terra / Rich Tobacco):** Used for tactile warmth, subtle badges, and secondary callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#C99700` - Aged Brass / Burnished Leaf):** Complements primary gold for hover states, gradient stops, and fine-line borders.
- **Neutral (`#121110` - Smoked Obsidian):** The foundational dark canvas. An obsidian base with microscopic warm brown undertones avoids cold digital grays.

### Named Accent Colors
- **Wrapper Tones (`#2B1A12`):** Surface containers, card backgrounds, and drawer overlays.
- **Spanish Cedar Tones (`#E8D5B5`, `#8E5B3D`):** High-contrast editorial text, warm labels, and organic dividers.
- **Vitola Foot Ribbons (`#A31621`, `#1B4D3E`):** Distinctive pops of color dedicated to blend identifiers (Habano Scarlet, Claro Emerald) echoing the ribbon feet seen on the cigars.

## Typography

Typography delivers an authoritative, high-craft editorial presence reminiscent of historic vitola bands and classic club publications:

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Elegant high-contrast transitional serif offering romantic drama, artisanal sophistication, and distinct editorial cadence for vitola releases, story chapters, and tasting profiles.
- **Body & Labels (`Cinzel`):** Derived from classical Roman inscriptional lettering with subtle proportions and open geometry, providing a timeless engraving feel.
- **Letter Spacing:** Headlines utilize tight to neutral tracking to project substance, while uppercase labels and technical metadata (ring gauge, wrapper type, origin) demand deliberate uppercase tracking (`0.14em` - `0.18em`).

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an open, expansive layout rhythm that prioritizes breathing space, framing, and quiet confidence:

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Fixed max-width of `1360px` with `4rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Elements snap along rigid architectural verticals.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid presentation with `2rem` outer margins.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Single and double-column stacked arrangements with `1.25rem` outer margins.

### Spacing Cadence
- Content sections rely on breathing margins (`space-xl` up to double `space-xl`) to emulate high-end print lookbooks.
- Vitola specification matrices, tasting grids, and provenance timelines use tight internal component gaps (`space-xs` to `space-md`) bounded by hairline rules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects buoyant, floating drop shadows in favor of grounded, textural surfaces, gold hairlines, and ambient backdrops:

- **Tonal Layering:** The primary canvas sits at `#121110`. Cards and elevated panels shift to `#1B1918` or `#2B1A12` (wrapper tone), creating depth through rich warmth rather than artificial light.
- **Gilded Hairline Framing:** Low-contrast hairline outlines (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.22)`) define boundaries, referencing hot-stamped gold foil. Hover states subtly intensify this gold opacity to `0.45`.
- **Atmospheric Amber Vignette:** Modals and feature highlights project an ultra-diffused, warm backlight tint (`0 20px 60px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)` combined with `0 0 40px rgba(138, 60, 27, 0.15)`).
- **Embossed Accent Elements:** Badges, medallions, and decorative insignia employ subtle inset bevels (`inset 0 1px 1px rgba(244, 208, 111, 0.3)`) to replicate stamped tin and embossed foil bands.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy is decisively sharp and architectural (`roundedness: 0`). 

- **Sharp Edges:** Boxes, cards, buttons, dropdowns, and imagery containers feature exact `0px` corners, evoking handcrafted cedar cigar boxes, humidors, and bevel-cut band stock.
- **Ornamental Exceptions:** Circular framing is reserved exclusively for the signature goat emblem medallion, ring gauge gauges, and wax seals.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Gold Foil Edition):** Solid `#D4AF37` fill with dark `#121110` Cinzel bold uppercase typography, tracked wide (`0.14em`). Zero border-radius. On hover, background transitions to `#F4D06F` with a soft gold glow.
- **Secondary Action (Atelier Outline):** Transparent background with `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.5)` border and `#EDE6D9` cedar-cream text. Hover fills the button with `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.1)`.
- **Text / Editorial Action:** Understated text with an animated bottom hairline that expands from center out on hover.

### Cards & Tasting Notes
- **Vitola Specimen Card:** Flat dark container `#1B1918` bounded by a subtle `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)` hairline. Includes a designated photography window showcasing the cigar against rich wood grain, topped by uppercase tasting tags (e.g., "MADURO 58X5", "HABANO 2023").
- **Tasting Profile Cards:** Quadrant layout highlighting Flavor Profile, Burn & Draw, Strength (1 to 5 scale shown as gold ticks), and Origin (Humo Real, Dominican Republic).

### Chips & Badges
- **Blend & Origin Chips:** Sharp rectangular tags with `1px` borders. Background utilizes translucent wrapper brown (`rgba(43, 26, 18, 0.6)`), text set in Cedar Cream (`#E8D5B5`), with small uppercase tracking.
- **Foot Ribbon Accent Tags:** Micro-badges utilizing `#A31621` (Crimson ribbon) or `#1B4D3E` (Emerald ribbon) denoting wrapper year and custom cellar lots.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Inputs:** Borderless top/sides with an emphatic bottom border (`1px solid rgba(232, 213, 181, 0.3)`). Background is sheer charcoal (`#161514`). Labels float above in uppercase Cinzel. Active focus illuminates the underline to metallic `#D4AF37`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Selectors:** Geometric sharp squares (`14px by 14px`) with `1px solid #D4AF37`. When checked, displays an inner solid gold square inlay rather than a digital checkmark icon.

### Bespoke Domain Components
- **Vitola Gauge Meter:** A horizontal gauge bar tracking Cigar Length and Ring Gauge using fine notched graduations.
- **Humidor Inventory Drawer:** Slide-out drawer with dark wood tone background (`#2B1A12`), gold-lined dividers, and status stamps ("Aging 18 Months", "Ready for Extraction").
